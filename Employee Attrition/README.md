# HR Employee Attrition Analysis
## Python Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## About the Dataset

**Source:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset (Kaggle)
**File:** WA_Fn-UseC_-HR-Employee-Attrition.csv

### Dataset Overview
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Total Features: 35 columns
- Target Variable: Attrition (Yes / No)
- Missing Values: None
- Duplicate Rows: None

### What This Dataset Contains
This is a fictional dataset created by IBM data scientists to understand 
what factors lead employees to leave an organization.

It covers 5 key areas of employee information:
1. Demographics       — Age, Gender, Marital Status, Education
2. Job Information    — Department, Job Role, Job Level, Business Travel
3. Compensation       — Monthly Income, Daily Rate, Salary Hike %
4. Satisfaction Score — Job Satisfaction, Work Life Balance, 
                        Environment Satisfaction, Relationship Satisfaction
5. Experience         — Years at Company, Years in Current Role, 
                        Total Working Years, No. of Companies Worked

### Business Problem
Employee attrition is costly for organizations — it impacts productivity, 
increases hiring costs, and affects team morale.

This analysis aims to answer:
- What is the overall attrition rate?
- Which departments and job roles are most affected?
- Does compensation influence attrition?
- Does overtime and work-life balance drive employees to leave?
- What employee profile is most at risk of attrition?
